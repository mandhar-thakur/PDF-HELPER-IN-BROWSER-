PDF

Merge, edit, split, and convert into PDFs — works today.

Merge PDFs

Pick two or more PDFs — they'll combine in the order selected.

Compress PDF

Redraws each page as an image at the quality you pick — big size cuts on scan- or photo-heavy PDFs. Note: text stops being selectable/searchable afterward, since pages become images.

Edit pages (delete / rotate / reorder)

Load one PDF, tweak pages below, then export.

Text, pen & eraser

Draw, stamp text, or whiteout content right on the page — a markup pass, not reflowable text editing. Exports pages as images, like Compress PDF.

Text tool: tap empty space to add text; drag existing text to move it; tap (without dragging) existing text to edit or clear it to delete.

100%

Split / extract pages

Enter a page range (e.g. 1-3,5) to pull out as a new PDF.

Images → PDF

Pick one or more images (JPG/PNG) — each becomes a page.

Text → PDF

Paste or type text, or load a .txt file, then convert.

Images

Compress and enhance photos — works today.

Compress images

Pick an image and a quality level — re-encodes as JPEG, usually much smaller.

Enhance image quality

Sharpens edges and can upscale a soft or low-res photo. This is a filter, not AI reconstruction — it won't invent detail that isn't there, but it noticeably crisps up soft/blurry shots.

Documents

Convert Word docs into PDF — works today (text only).

Word (.docx) → PDF

Older .doc files aren't supported, only .docx.

Runs entirely in your browser — files never leave your device.
